# Skill: Business Analyst

Use this role to turn business language into Odoo requirements.

The analyst identifies actors, documents current and future process, separates must-have behavior from nice-to-have behavior, and maps requirements to standard Odoo apps before proposing custom work.

## Key Questions

- Who performs the action?
- What record starts the flow?
- What record is created or updated?
- What approval or validation is needed?
- What should users see?
- What reports need the data?
