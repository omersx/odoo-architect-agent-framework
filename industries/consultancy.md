# Industry Playbook: Consultancy

Consultancy work sells time, deliverables, retainers, and service agreements.

## Recommended Extension

`biz_bridge_consultancy`

## Dependencies

- `biz_bridge_pro`
- `hr_timesheet`
- `sale_timesheet`

## Features

- Assign consultants per sale order line.
- Create tasks assigned to the selected consultant.
- Replace standard quotation layout with a service agreement report.
- Track delivered hours and remaining budget.

## Risks

- Task generation depends on product service tracking configuration.
- Timesheet access and employee privacy require review.
