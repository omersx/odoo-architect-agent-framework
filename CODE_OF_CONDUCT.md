# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project welcomes respectful, constructive participation from the Odoo and AI-agent community.

Participants should:

- Be respectful and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code.
- Help newcomers understand Odoo and framework conventions.
- Avoid harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who repeatedly disrupt the project.
