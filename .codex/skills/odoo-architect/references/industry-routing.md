# Industry Routing

Use the hub-and-spoke model.

## Construction

Focus on project delivery, site location, analytic costs, procurement, and job costing.

## Pharmacy

Focus on lots, expiry, controlled products, patient or doctor references, and POS receipts.

## Ecommerce

Focus on checkout, website controllers, portal visibility, delivery, and payment/accounting handoff.

## Retail

Focus on POS speed, receipt output, offline sync, stock visibility, and simple urgent order actions.

## Consultancy

Focus on service products, task creation, consultant assignment, timesheets, and service agreement reports.
